What's new in 1.3

  • Lifetime license. DotStrip is now a one-time purchase, $9.99, with no subscription. If you already subscribe, nothing changes for you.
  • Pink. A new hot-pink color for the board, in the neon-sign spirit.
  • One band, everything scrolling. The shared band is now one continuous line that every widget takes its turn in: countdowns, now playing, weather, stocks and headlines, so you can have them all on at once.
  • Custom timers. A new Timers widget. Start a preset from the board or the menu, add your own lengths like 5m or 1h30m, and watch the countdown on the strip.
  • Drag to resize. Drag the seams between widgets, or between a graphic and the band, to make each one as wide as you want. Your sizes are remembered, and Reset Width puts them back.
  • Smoother resizing. Resizing no longer lags while you drag.
  • High Contrast mode. A brighter, bolder board that is easier to read.
  • A tidier menu. Settings are grouped under Board, Appearance and Widget Settings instead of one long list.
