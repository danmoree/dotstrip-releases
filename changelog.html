Changelog

What's new.

Every version of DotStrip, newest first. The app updates itself: choose Check for Updates from its menu to get the latest.

1.33 November 2026

New

  • Lifetime license. DotStrip is now a one-time purchase, $9.99, with no subscription. If you already subscribe, nothing changes for you. Lifetime license keys can only be redeemed in version 1.3 or later, so update older versions first.
  • Pink. A new hot-pink color for the board, in the neon-sign spirit. See it on the board.
  • Custom timers. A new Timers widget. Start a preset from the board or the menu, add your own lengths like 5m or 1h30m, and watch the countdown on the strip.
  • Drag to resize. Drag the seams between widgets, or between a graphic and the band, to make each one as wide as you want. Your sizes are remembered, and Reset Width puts them back.
  • High Contrast mode. A deeper black background, so the lit dots stand out and the board is easier to read.

Improved

  • One band, everything scrolling. The shared band is now one continuous line that every widget takes its turn in: countdowns, now playing, weather, stocks and headlines, so you can have them all on at once.
  • A tidier menu. Settings are grouped under Board, Appearance and Widget Settings instead of one long list.
  • Smoother resizing. Resizing no longer lags while you drag.

Fixed

  • A bug when resizing widgets or the strip by hand.
1.22 October 2026

Improved

  • Apple Music. Now Playing asks every running player and shows the one that is playing, so a paused Spotify no longer hides Apple Music. Streams and stations with no album or artist show up too.
  • More characters. Curly quotes, accented letters and a run of symbols used to draw as a placeholder box. They are drawn properly now.
  • Denser dots when HDR is off, so the sign no longer reads as grey.

New

  • The menu shows the version and build, and there is a Launch at Login item, off by default.
1.12 October 2026

The first public release. Now Playing, News, Stocks and Weather on a dot-matrix board in your menu bar, Graphics mode, five colors, HDR Glow, a 7-day free trial and automatic updates.
