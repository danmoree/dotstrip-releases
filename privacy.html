Privacy policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

DotStrip is a Mac app that runs on your computer, made by Daniel Moreno, an individual (“I”). The app has no accounts and no analytics, and it does not send me anything about you. I do not sell your personal information or share it for advertising. The only personal information I receive is what you give me when you buy or email me, described below.

What the app contacts

To do its job the app makes requests from your Mac to these services. They see your IP address and the request itself, as with any website, and handle it under their own privacy policies:

  • Open-Meteo for weather and for searching place names. The place you pick, and what you type to search for it, is sent to it. The app never reads your device location.
  • Yahoo Finance for stock quotes. The symbols you track are sent to it.
  • RSS or Atom feeds you choose, which are fetched from whichever site hosts them.
  • This site, which is hosted on GitHub Pages, to check for app updates (an appcast file) and to download them. The update check includes the app version and your macOS version and chip type, as an ordinary software update does. It does not send a hardware profile, and I do not link it to you.
  • Polar, to check your license key (see below).

Stored on your Mac

Your settings, tracked symbols, chosen place, feeds and timers are stored on your Mac and are not sent to me. Your license key and trial start date are stored in your Mac’s Keychain. Deleting the app and its Keychain entries removes them.

Now Playing

The track title and artist are read locally from Spotify or Music on your Mac using Apple Events, only after you allow it. They are never sent anywhere.

Payments and license keys

Purchases are handled by Polar, which acts as the merchant of record. It collects what it needs to take payment and issue a license key, such as your email address, name and country, and its privacy policy applies to that. I do not see your card details. Polar gives me order information, such as your email address and what you bought, which I use only to provide your license, give support and meet tax and legal duties. The app sends your license key to Polar when it starts so it can confirm that the key is valid.

Email

If you email me, I receive your address and what you write, and I use it only to answer you. I keep support emails for as long as they are useful to you or me, or as the law requires, and then delete them.

This website

This website uses Umami, a privacy-friendly analytics service, to count page views and clicks on the Download and Buy buttons. According to Umami, it sets no cookies and does not track you across sites or store your IP address. The site is hosted by GitHub Pages, which keeps its own server logs. The site remembers your color choice in your browser’s local storage, which never leaves your device.

Why I handle this information

I use the information above to provide the app and your license (to perform our contract), to run and secure the site and to answer you (my legitimate interests), and to meet legal obligations. Reading Now Playing happens only if you consent to the macOS Automation prompt.

Your rights

You can ask me to tell you what personal information I hold about you, and to correct or delete it. Depending on where you live (for example the EU, the UK or California) you may have further rights, such as to object, to restrict processing, or to complain to your data protection authority. Email me and I will respond within 30 days. Information held by Polar, GitHub, Umami and the other services above is controlled by them, so ask them directly for that.

Retention and where it goes

I keep order records for as long as needed for your license, support, taxes and legal claims. The services above are mostly based in the United States, so information about you may be handled there.

Children

DotStrip is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you think a child has sent me any, email me and I will delete it.

Changes

If I change this policy I will update the date above and, for a significant change, mention it in the changelog.
